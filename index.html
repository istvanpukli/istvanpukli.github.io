What I can do for you

I design products where getting it wrong has consequences: regulated, multi-market and technically complex.

  1. 1. Regulated markets

    KYC, identity verification and responsible-use flows that meet the rule without turning it into friction.

  2. 2. Multi-market products

    Adapting flows to each jurisdiction’s regulator, language and legal requirements while keeping one shared product core.

  3. 3. Reusable systems

    Shared components and themeable design systems, so the next market, brand or feature doesn’t start from zero.

  4. 4. Design with a builder’s mind

    A QA and front-end background: I prototype in code, write specs engineers can build from, review builds against the design and test my own flows before they ship.
